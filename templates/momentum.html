Understanding Momentum Alpha & Trend Philosophy (Click to expand)

1. What is Momentum Alpha?

Momentum is a premier empirical asset-pricing anomaly. It describes the tendency for assets with strong relative performance over the medium term (3-12 months) to continue outperforming, while weak assets continue to lag. Momentum forms the mathematical core of Commodity Trading Advisor (CTA) trend-following models.

2. The 12-1 Momentum Rule

To calculate momentum scores, we measure return over the past 12 months (252 trading days) but exclude the most recent 1 month (21 trading days). Short-term returns (1-30 days) are heavily driven by liquidity flows, market impact, and short-term exhaustion, resulting in high mean-reversion. Stripping out the last 21 days isolates the clean, medium-term structural trend.

3. Volatility-Scaled (Risk-Adjusted) Momentum

Ranks are determined using a Risk-Adjusted Momentum Score, computed by dividing the 12-1 momentum return by the asset's annualized standard deviation (volatility) over the same period. This ensures that stable, persistent uptrends are prioritized over erratic, high-volatility spikes that may crash unpredictably.

4. Time-Series Strategy & Frictions

The strategy backtested on this page simulates a Time-Series Momentum model. It holds the stock long when its 12-1 momentum score is positive (>0%), and drops to 100% Cash (flat) during bearish crossovers. Frictional drag is accounted for by charging a realistic 7.5 basis points round-trip execution fee on all traded notional.

CTA Trend-Following Philosophy

📉 Drawdown Shielding: In times of market crises, individual stocks can suffer massive, structural drawdowns. The Time-Series Trend strategy is designed to go Flat (Cash) once the momentum score crosses below 0%, shielding the portfolio from extended bear markets.

🏎️ Trend Capture: By entering only when the momentum score becomes positive, the strategy captures the bulk of structural multi-year bull runs (such as the recent technology rally) while ignoring sideways noise.
